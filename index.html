SSIPThe Health and Safety section assessed on its own. Holding it stands the CAS H&S questions down.
CAS (full)The whole standard, all ten sections, gates applied.
Constructionline GoldAssessed against the full standard. Any Gold-specific extras beyond CAS are to be verified from the Constructionline documents.
BSA HRBSection 7 in full, plus questions 151-154 which apply only where Higher-Risk Building work is carried out.
Gate questions - what changes the scope
Q21Employs sub-contractors? No: questions 38, 116, 130, 139, 160, 165 and 166 stand down.
Q22In-scope BSA building or design work? No: all of Section 7 (Q135-154) stands down.
Q23Higher-Risk Building work? No: Q151-154 stand down.
Q24Projects with a structured Information Management requirement? No: Section 10 (Q170-176) stands down.
Q71SSIP or ISO 45001 held? Yes: Q73-85 and Q86-110 stand down - H&S is evidenced by the certificate.
Q89CDM dutyholder roles held - drives which of Q90-110 apply, per role.
Q111ISO 14001 or EMAS held? Yes: Q112-116 stand down.
Q122BS 99001 held? Yes: Q125-134 and Section 7 stand down.
Q123ISO 9001 held? Yes: Q125-134 stand down.
Q142BSA dutyholder roles held - drives which of Q143-154 apply, per role.

Scope and gate rules follow the Common Assessment Standard framework verified in Compass on 2 July 2026. Question numbers reference the standard; wording here is summary, not source text.
